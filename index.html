About the Summit

A Platform for Young Opportunity Seekers

Bringing young people together to discover opportunities, exchange ideas, and grow.

What is VIOS Summit 2026?

The Vijana International Opportunities Summit (VIOS) is a dynamic gathering designed for young people who are ready to dream bigger, learn faster, and connect with opportunities that shape their future.

Whether you are a student, innovator, aspiring entrepreneur, artist, or someone seeking international study and work opportunities, VIOS Summit creates a space to explore, network, and take the next step in your journey.

What to Expect

Summit Activities

A full day of learning, entertainment, competition and connection.

Jogging

Start the day with energy. Join fellow participants for a morning run and wellness activity.

Brass Band

Live brass band performances that set an energetic and celebratory atmosphere throughout the event.

Artists & Entertainment

Experience talented artists and entertainers who will keep the energy high and the vibes positive.

Games

Fun interactive games and competitions designed to break the ice and build teamwork.

Panel Discussions

Thought-provoking conversations on opportunities, careers, innovation and youth development.

Startups & Innovators

Meet young founders and innovators. Discover ideas and projects shaping the future.

Abroad Jobs & Study

Explore international study programmes, scholarships and job opportunities beyond Tanzania.

Volunteering

Learn about meaningful volunteering opportunities and how you can contribute to your community.

Networking

Connect with peers, professionals and opportunity providers in a relaxed and open environment.
